🚗 EdgeDrive3D Technical Architecture

Cost-Effective Autonomous Navigation System for Developing Regions

High-Cost Sensors

Traditional autonomous systems rely on expensive LiDAR sensors ($1,000-$75,000), making them inaccessible for developing regions and small-scale deployments.

Lack of Infrastructure

Most developing regions lack proper road monitoring infrastructure, real-time traffic data, and high-definition mapping systems required for autonomous navigation.

Cost Prohibitive

Complete autonomous navigation systems cost $50,000+, making them impractical for widespread adoption in budget-constrained environments.

  • NVIDIA Jetson Nano/Xavier (GPU Accelerated)
  • Low Latency (< 100ms)
  • No Internet Dependency
  • Lower Operating Cost
  • Privacy: Data stays on device
